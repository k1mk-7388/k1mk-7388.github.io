Amateur Radio Contest Pages

Planning and analysis by K1MK for K1SFA at K1TTT (FN32). Each contest has its propagation prediction, band plans and operating sheets; the contest pages index lists them all.

JARL WW RTTY Contest 2026

Propagation and plan for 17–18 October — the contest that ran as JARTS through 2025. Score is points times multipliers, a multiplier is worth about four QSOs, and four years of this station's own logs say 80m is the most score-dense band per QSO despite producing the fewest points.

 Makrothen RTTY Contest 2026

Propagation and plan for 10–11 October: distance is the score and there are no multipliers, so the band plan is by grid field with each QSO priced in kilometers times its band factor. Includes what four years of this station's own logs say about where the points actually come from.

 CQ WW RTTY DX Contest 2026

Propagation prediction for 26–27 September: the band plan by CQ zone, operating sheets, five great-circle animations, and what 17 years of public logs say 10m does at this solar flux.

 WW Digi DX Contest 2026

Station plan for 29–30 August: operating sheets, multiplier plan, band plans and band animations.
